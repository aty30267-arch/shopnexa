Buying Guides

How to choose a phone

Start with your budget and use case. Compare processor performance, camera hardware, display, battery, storage, update policy and service availability. Do not choose on headline specifications alone.

How to choose a laptop

Consider workload first: study, office work, coding, editing or gaming. Then compare CPU/GPU, RAM, SSD, display, thermals, ports, weight and warranty.

How to choose earbuds

Look at comfort, ANC, microphone quality, battery, multipoint support, codec compatibility and app support. Reviews should match your actual use case.
